# Code of conduct

GitIsle follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and take
feedback on the work rather than the person, and keep harassment, insults,
and discrimination out of every project space, including issues, pull
requests, and discussions.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately
through [GitHub](https://github.com/ppazosp). Reports are handled in
confidence. Maintainers may remove content or block people who don't follow
this code.
