## Overview

Bespoke spec (no shipped preset fits): the same system as the landing page in
`site/`. Black, white and GitHub's state colours, Geist type, an Apple-style
product sting.

## The frame

The canvas is a MacBook screen. The real island (recorded from the app) hangs
from the top edge, centred, over the landing page's dark wallpaper with a soft
green and purple glow. A camera scales the screen around the top-centre point:
tight on the notch for pills and banners, pulled back for the expanded island.

## Composition rules

- Real app footage only for the product; never a rebuilt imitation.
- One short line of copy at a time. Captions sit centred under a small island,
  or along the bottom under an expanded one.
- Motion is quick and eased (expo/power out); cuts land on the music's bars.
