## Intent

The launch video for GitIsle 0.2. The first cut used still captures and felt
repetitive; the user asked for "more motion, more movement like the first one"
and a cool techno track from Mixkit. So, like the 0.1 video, it is built on real
footage of the island animating, with one camera moving over it, type that
blurs in on the bars, and cuts on the music.

## Assets

- assets/island-tour.webm — the real app driven by `--tour pages`, recorded with transparency.
- assets/share-card.jpg — a share card for a merged PR, rendered by the app.
- assets/logo.png — the GitIsle mark.
- audio/ — Mixkit "Techno Fest Feel" (track 125), downloaded, not committed.

## Notes

- No narration: on-screen type carries the story, with the music.
- Example data only (orbit/web, orbit/api); the share card is the user's own merged PR.
